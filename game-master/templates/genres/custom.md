# Rules: custom

The ruleset for this game. It builds on the table rules in the project's
`CLAUDE.md` (checks, difficulty ladder, outcome tiers, Fortune, clocks,
conditions). Fill each section during session zero; the other genre files in
`templates/genres/` show how much detail is enough. Record later changes
under House rules in `game.md`.

## Where the challenge comes from

One paragraph: what is scarce, who opposes the character, and what can be
lost. Every other section should serve this.

## Skills

Six to eight skills rated 0 to 5, each with a line on what it covers, and the
array a new character assigns (for eight skills: 4, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1, 0, 0).

## Resources

Two to four tracks or stocks with small ranges, what each is spent on, how it
is regained, and what happens at zero.

## Time

The unit of the turn (watch, day, week, month), how many significant actions
fit in one, and the order of a turn.

## The world turn

When it runs and its steps: who acts, how their progress is rolled, what
random event table is used, and how news of it reaches the character.

## Conflict

How the genre's typical high-stakes contest is resolved in a few checks with
stated stakes.

## Losing

What failure usually costs, and what the worst case is.

## Advancement

When and how the character improves.
